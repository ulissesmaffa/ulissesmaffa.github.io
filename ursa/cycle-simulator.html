Model: one word per cycle on each port after a burst starts; the FIFO depth and the burst latency are the two knobs that are not in the IP (the IP has depth 32, and the latency depends on the memory mapping). Values of A are int8 in −2..2, values of B are uint8 in 0..3, so every number fits on screen; C is checked against A·B at the end of the run. The array is simulated PE by PE: operand a travels right and b travels down one PE per cycle, and the skew shift registers of compute delay row i by i cycles and column j by j cycles, exactly as in shell.cpp.
