Tools and simulators

URSA v3: interactive cycle-level simulators

URSA is an HLS systolic-array GEMM accelerator for the Zynq-7000. These pages step through its v3 streaming shell one clock cycle at a time, from the outside in.

  • URSA v3 cycle simulator (English): the whole shell: read_a, read_b, compute and write_c as four concurrent processes, the FIFOs between them, the output-stationary array, the shadow registers and the tile-major C.
  • Abastecendo as filas (Portuguese): who feeds the FIFOs s_a and s_b: the AXI ports, bursts and their latency, read_a with its a_buf replay, read_b without a buffer, and compute as the consumer.
  • Caminho do dado no URSA v3 (Portuguese): from the memory word to each PE accumulator: FIFOs, skew registers, array edges, the PEs, the shadow copy and the write of C.
  • Alimentando a borda do array (Portuguese): the interface between shell and array only: the word popped from the FIFO, the skew chains dA and dB, the edge registers and what each PE multiplies at each step.
